Frequently Asked Questions

AgriTrust is an integrated digital platform that connects farmers directly with buyers, food processors, and exporters. It ensures fair pricing by eliminating middlemen, provides access to markets, and offers services like logistics, quality testing, and financial support, thereby increasing farmer income and stability.
Our platform encourages sustainable practices by providing farmers with access to information on organic farming, water conservation, and soil health management. We also have a "Residue to Resource" program that helps farmers convert agricultural waste into valuable products like bio-fuels and organic fertilizers, creating an additional revenue stream and reducing environmental pollution.
Registration on the AgriTrust platform is completely free for farmers. We believe in empowering the farming community without imposing upfront financial burdens. Our revenue model is based on a small, transparent commission on successful transactions, ensuring we only succeed when our farmers do.
AgriTrust mitigates market risks through long-term contracts with buyers and price-locking mechanisms. For weather-related risks, we have partnered with leading insurance companies to offer tailored crop insurance products. Additionally, our platform provides real-time weather forecasts and advisories to help farmers take preventive measures.
